# URC 2024-25 Inclusion Methodology

> Generated by `tools/replay.py --write-methodology-only`. Do not edit by hand.

## Three-layer contract

1. **Master:** Preserves every standardized source row and records row-level exclusions. It does not store inclusion-only inference.
2. **Decision ledger:** Applies ordered, evidence-bound corrections, standardizations, inferences, adjudications, and inclusion removals.
3. **Inclusion:** Contains only master rows with a blank exclusion reason after the ordered ledger is replayed. Analysis consumes this layer.

## Reporting cohort boundary: v5

The accepted 2024-25 inclusion lineage remains unchanged: the 3,060-row
master, ordered decision ledger, and accepted 2,301-row inclusion CSV are
not rebuilt, re-cleaned, or regenerated for this reporting-cohort rule.
The v5 analysis layer instead applies an immutable effective eligibility
view for the inclusive reporting window 1 September 2024 to 30 June 2025.

Dated injuries before 1 September are valid cleaned records but are outside
the v5 reporting cohort. Six undated, season-attributed injuries remain in
applicable totals and non-monthly breakdowns, but remain outside monthly
series. All v5 monthly readers bind to the same v5 cohort identity as the
headline and other cohort-derived readers.

Exposure is also evaluated at row level without mutating curated rows. A row
is re-admitted only where `outside_official_analysis_window` is its sole
historical inclusion blocker and its reporting period overlaps the v5 window.
All other historical exclusions remain effective. Weekly rows retain their
native grain and use their recorded start date plus six calendar days for
period overlap.

Only in the newly opened 1 to 19 September pre-URC band, clearly evidenced
non-URC match, friendly, or opponent-fixture activity is excluded. Ambiguous
labels, warm-ups, top-ups, captain's runs, `Game -N` training-cycle labels,
and blank labels remain included unless other evidence is decisive. This
semantic rule does not alter existing-window rows or generalise across the
league or season. Fixture-derived match hours remain 6,040.

The rule framework carries forward only through a versioned successor view
and a newly registered window for each season. The 2024-25 dates and its
pre-URC semantic evidence are not silently reused for another season.
See `docs/evidence/analysis_window_2024-25_v5.json` and
`docs/ANALYSIS_WINDOW_2024-25_BEFORE_AFTER.md` for the accepted scope and
reconciliation contract.
